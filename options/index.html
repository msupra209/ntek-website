Why the logo is untouched this time

The mark was rendered on five grounds and inspected before either option was designed. On #091E81 and on near-black, the word "Solutions" disappears entirely and the K's leg breaks out of the light ellipse. The mark only holds on light grounds.

So Option B does not recolour the logo — it gives it a bone header bar and bone footer. Neither option ships a dark logo variant, and neither lightens #091E81 away from its true value.
