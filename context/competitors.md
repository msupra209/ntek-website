# NTeK Solutions Website — Competitors

Research list for the revamp — a mix of direct regional competitors (same geography, similar scale) and international benchmarks (larger scale or specifically well-reviewed MSP website design, useful for the story/UX direction work in `revamp-plan.md`).

## Local / Regional (direct competitors — same GCC/MENA market)

| # | Company | Site | Why it's on this list |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **KuwaitNet** | [kuwaitnet.com](https://kuwaitnet.com) | Kuwait-based, 25 years in market, Red Hat partner — closest direct local peer to NteK on tenure and positioning. |
| 2 | **GBM (Gulf Business Machines)** | [gbmme.com](https://www.gbmme.com) | Major GCC digital-infrastructure/managed-services provider, present in Kuwait via KBM. Bigger scale (1500+ employees, 7 GCC offices) — the aspirational end of the regional comp set. |
| 3 | **Intertec Systems** | [intertecsystems.com](https://www.intertecsystems.com) | MENA managed services (UAE/Oman/KSA/Qatar/Bahrain/India), ISO 27001 + ISO 20000 certified, SLA-driven positioning — good comp for how a regional MSP communicates trust/compliance. |
| 4 | **NavLink** | [navlink.com](https://www.navlink.com) | Managed hosting, security, and public cloud from GCC datacenters including Kuwait, in-country NOCs — closest functional match to NteK's DRaaS/cloud/backup lines specifically. |
| 5 | **Softenger** | [softenger.com](https://softenger.com) | Dubai-anchored, managed IT governance extending across Saudi, Egypt, Bahrain, Kuwait under one SLA framework — same "one regional operator, many countries" story NteK needs to tell. |

## International (benchmark for scale, credibility signals, or web design specifically)

| # | Company | Site | Why it's on this list |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | **Kyndryl** | [kyndryl.com](https://www.kyndryl.com) | IBM's managed-infrastructure spinoff — the global-enterprise ceiling for how a pure managed-services company presents credibility at scale. |

| 10 | **Synoptek** | [synoptek.com](https://synoptek.com) | Coined its own category ("Managed Experience Provider") to differentiate in a crowded MSP market — directly relevant to NteK's own differentiation problem, and repeatedly cited as one of the better-designed MSP marketing sites. |

## How to use this list

- **Local (1–5):** audit for what they claim, how they present coverage/SLA, and whether any have real client logos/case studies published (NteK currently doesn't — flagged as a gap in `revamp-plan.md`).
- **International (6–10):** audit for information architecture (how a broad service portfolio is organized so it doesn't read as a flat list — ties to the "exploded stack" idea in the story-direction artifact) and for how credibility is signaled without inflating claims.
- Not yet done: side-by-side screenshots/notes per site. Suggested next step if this research continues — one pass per site capturing hero treatment, IA, proof points used, and one thing worth stealing.
