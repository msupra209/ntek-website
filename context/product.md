# NTeK Solutions Website — Product

## Overview
Corporate/marketing website for NTeK Solutions, a managed IT services provider headquartered in Kuwait, operating since 2004 across 14 MENA + Pakistan markets. The site is the primary digital front door for enterprise and government buyers evaluating NTeK as an MSP, and a credibility signal for channel partners (AWS, Acronis, Infrascale, Oracle).

## Problem
Current site (nteksolutions.net, Wix-built) undersells a 20-year regional operator: generic stock photography, thin/templated copy, no case studies or proof points, no team/leadership presence, weak differentiation from other MENA MSPs, dead-end CTAs ("Learn More" → same page), phone-only contact with no working lead form, flat visual hierarchy. It looks like a small local shop, not the operator behind 70+ infrastructure projects across 14 countries.

## Target Users
Four distinct audiences, each with different content needs:
1. **IT/procurement decision-makers** (CIOs, IT managers) at mid-large enterprises — need service depth, SLAs, coverage proof, security/compliance posture.
2. **Government/public sector buyers** — formal RFP/tender process, need credentials, certifications, references, compliance documentation to be findable and citable.
3. **SMBs** — price/service-sensitive, need clarity on what "managed IT" actually gets them, simpler on-ramp to contact.
4. **Channel partners** (AWS, Acronis, Infrascale, Oracle, regional resellers) — need partner-tier visibility, not sales-funnel content.

## Core Flows
1. Land on homepage → understand NTeK is a credible, established regional MSP within 5 seconds.
2. Browse service lines (9 total: Field Engineering, Infrastructure, On-Demand Support, Managed IT, Managed Cloud/AWS, DRaaS, On-Premise Backup, Software Solutions, Oracle DBA) → find the one relevant to their need.
3. Check coverage region/country-level detail → confirm NTeK operates where they need support.
4. Submit an inquiry via working contact form (not just phone numbers) → receive confirmation.
5. Government/enterprise buyer → find proof points (certifications, partner logos, project count, years in operation) without digging.

## Product Principles
1. **Credibility over cleverness** — this is a 20-year regional operator; the site should read as established infrastructure, not a startup landing page.
2. **Audience-routed, not audience-blended** — enterprise, government, SMB, and partner visitors have different jobs-to-be-done; avoid one generic page trying to serve all four.
3. **Proof over adjectives** — replace "best-in-class," "innovative," "quality" with numbers, logos, certifications, named clients where permitted.
4. **Regional specificity is the differentiator** — MENA + Pakistan coverage with field engineers deployable in 1-2 hours is a real operational advantage; make it concrete (map, country-level SLA table), not a paragraph claim.

## What Exists Today
